# Dashwise

Dashwise turns your Android phone into a dashcam. It keeps recording under Waze and Maps, saves the moment after a bump, stamps time, speed and address on the video and logs every trip on a map. No adverts, no account, no tracking.

Website: https://customgpter.github.io/dashwise/

Download: https://github.com/CustomGPTer/dashwise/releases/latest/download/Dashwise.apk

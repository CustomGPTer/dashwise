Release notes

What changed in each version of Dashwise. The newest is at the top.

Version 1.2.1 22 November 2026Latest

  • Night dimming you can set. Two new sliders under Settings, Protection: Dim sensitivity sets how dark it must get before the screen dims, and Dimmed brightness sets how bright the screen stays once dimmed.
  • New defaults: dimming now waits for slightly darker conditions, and the dimmed screen sits at 20% instead of 8% so it stays easy to read.
  • Changes take effect straight away, without leaving the screen.

Version 1.2.0 22 November 2026

  • First public release. Records in the background under Waze, Maps or any app, with the screen off or the phone locked.
  • A floating indicator over every app while recording, with Save, Photo, Mute, Open and Stop. Choose its style, size and see-through level.
  • Loop recording in 1 to 10 minute clips with a storage limit; saved clips are kept separately and never deleted automatically.
  • One-tap Save that keeps the lead-up, plus impact, hard-braking and swerve detection that saves clips by itself.
  • Date, time, speed, address, coordinates and your own text burned into the video.
  • Every lens with its angle of view, 720p to 4K, 60 fps, bitrate and stabilisation, and front-and-back recording on phones that support it.
  • A trip journal with route maps coloured by speed, a speed graph, events, GPX export and a map that follows the video as it plays.
  • An evidence pack PDF with key frames, the route and a SHA-256 check code, and a quick editor to trim, mute and pixelate an area.
  • Automatic start and stop with the car's Bluetooth or the charger, a widget, a quick-settings tile and app shortcuts.
  • Heat and battery protection, night dimming, a screen saver, optional speed warning and optional parking guard.
  • Optional backup of saved clips to your own Google Drive, on Wi-Fi only by default.

Checking your download

The file for version 1.2.1 is called Dashwise.apk. It is 5.9 MB (6,217,819 bytes). Its SHA-256 checksum is:

b8237ff866bae119008a03d30362b2e41389a4f50a6d1f89472f63a03ced51f5

On Windows, run certutil -hashfile Dashwise.apk SHA256 and compare. Every version is signed with the same key, so each one installs over the last and keeps what you have saved.

Older versions are on the GitHub releases page.

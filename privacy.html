Privacy policy

Last updated 22 November 2026. This policy covers the Dashwise app for Android (package uk.jj.dashwise) and this website. Dashwise is made by Jonathan Jackson, an individual developer in the United Kingdom.

The short version

  • There is no account, no adverts, no analytics and no tracking. The developer receives nothing from the app.
  • Your videos, photos and trips are stored on your phone.
  • Backup to Google Drive is off until you turn it on, and then goes only to your own Drive.
  • Map pictures, address lookups and a daily version check are the only other connections.

Who the app talks to, and when

ServiceWhat it receivesWhen
Your phone's address lookup (Google)Your position, to turn it into a street addressWhile recording with the address shown, at most every few seconds
CARTO and OpenStreetMap map tilesYour IP address and the map area of a trip you openOnly when you open a trip or play a clip with its map
GitHubYour IP address and a request for the newest Dashwise versionOnce a day, if "Tell me about updates" is on
Google Drive (your account)Your saved clips, into a Dashwise folderOnly if you turn backup on and sign in

Each service handles what it receives under its own policy. There is no Dashwise server, so nothing is sent to the developer.

Google Drive backup

If you turn backup on, you sign in with Google and allow Dashwise the narrow drive.file permission. That lets it create a Dashwise folder and see only the files it put there, nothing else in your Drive. Dashwise's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements. You can turn backup off at any time, and remove Dashwise's access in your Google account settings.

Location

Dashwise uses your precise location while it is recording, for the speed and address on the video and for the trip journal. Location is not used when Dashwise is closed and not recording. Trips are stored only on your phone.

Camera and microphone

The camera records only while you, or an automatic start you set up, are recording, and a notification is shown the whole time. Cabin sound is recorded unless you turn it off or mute it. Recordings are stored in your phone's gallery, where you control them.

What the app can reach

Camera, microphone, precise location, notifications, showing over other apps (for the floating indicator and automatic starts), running without battery limits, Bluetooth (only to recognise the car you choose), starting after a restart (only for the charger watcher, if you turn it on), vibration and internet access. No contacts, calls, texts or account access.

Deleting your data

Delete clips and trips in the Library. Uninstalling Dashwise removes its trips and settings; your videos stay in Movies/Dashwise until you delete them, because they are yours.

This website

This site is hosted by GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies and no analytics. GitHub may keep standard web server logs, such as IP addresses, for security, under GitHub's privacy statement.

Children

Dashwise collects nothing from anyone. It is meant for drivers.

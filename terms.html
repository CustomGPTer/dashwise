Driving safely

Set Dashwise up before you drive and mount the phone securely in a holder that does not block your view. In the UK it is illegal to hold and use a phone while driving, and the Highway Code (rule 149) asks you not to be distracted by a device. Never touch the phone in a way that takes your attention from the road.

Recording within the law

Recording the road from your own vehicle for your own use is lawful in the UK. If you share footage that shows identifiable people, or use Dashwise for work, data protection law (UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018) may apply. Tell passengers that sound is being recorded, or turn sound off. Laws differ in other countries; if you are not in the UK, the rules where you are apply.

Footage and evidence

Dashwise does its best to record and keep every clip, but phones can run out of space, overheat, be switched off or stop apps. Dashwise is not a certified recording device. Speed and position come from GPS and can be wrong. The check code in an evidence pack shows whether a file has changed; it does not prove what the video shows.

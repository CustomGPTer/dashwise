First public release of Dashwise, a free, private dashcam app for Android 10 or later.

- Records in the background under Waze, Maps or any app, with the screen off or the phone locked.
- A floating indicator over every app while recording, with Save, Photo, Mute, Open and Stop.
- Loop recording in 1 to 10 minute clips with a storage limit; saved clips are never deleted automatically.
- One-tap Save that keeps the lead-up, plus impact, hard-braking and swerve detection that saves clips by itself.
- Date, time, speed, address, coordinates and your own text burned into the video.
- Every lens with its angle of view, 720p to 4K, 60 fps, stabilisation, and front-and-back recording on phones that support it.
- A trip journal with route maps coloured by speed, a speed graph, events, GPX export and a map that follows the video.
- An evidence pack PDF with key frames, the route and a SHA-256 check code, and a quick editor to trim, mute and pixelate an area.
- Automatic start and stop with the car's Bluetooth or the charger, a widget, a quick-settings tile and app shortcuts.
- Heat and battery protection, night dimming, a screen saver, optional speed warning and optional parking guard.
- Optional backup of saved clips to your own Google Drive, on Wi-Fi only by default.

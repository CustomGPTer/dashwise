Dashwise 1.2.1: night dimming you can set.

- Two new sliders under Settings, Protection: Dim sensitivity sets how dark it must get before the screen dims, and Dimmed brightness sets how bright the screen stays once dimmed.
- New defaults: dimming now waits for slightly darker conditions, and the dimmed screen sits at 20% instead of 8% so it stays easy to read.
- Changes take effect straight away, without leaving the screen.

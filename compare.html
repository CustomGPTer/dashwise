What Dashwise does not do

Dashwise is not on Google Play, so you install it from this site. It has no live remote viewing, no speed-camera alerts and no AI object detection. Waze already covers cameras.

Where the others do more

DriveSight offers police and speed-camera alerts, live remote viewing and AI detection as part of Pro. Droid Dashcam has been on Google Play since 2020 with over a million installs.

A phone is not a hardware dashcam

Phones get hot and can be switched off or stop apps. Dashwise protects against heat and low battery, but a hard-wired dashcam is still more robust for parking footage.
